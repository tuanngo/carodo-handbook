# CARODO product vision

CARODO is a drive journal for people who enjoy driving: it records your drives, lets you relive
them as a replay, and lets you share them. Think "Strava for drives", private by default: the community Feed shows how people drive (car,
time, distance), never where (ADR 0007).

Product loop:

`Drive (auto or manual) → Journal → Replay → Share`

with the **Garage** giving every drive its car.

The first release focuses on reliable recording (Auto Mode first, manual always available), a 2D
replay, a share card and share link, and the garage. 3D flyover and video export come next.
Tabs: Map · Journeys · Feed, plus a floating Start Drive button. Parking is not a feature (ADR 0001); later a car's page may show where it was last parked.
Scope of the first release: [`carodo-ios/docs/specs/product.md`](../../carodo-ios/docs/specs/product.md).

## Positioning

A personal driving journal with the clarity of a vehicle dashboard and the emotional value of a
travel journal.

## Experience principles

1. **Frictionless:** no account is needed to start.
2. **Trustworthy:** a drive is not lost when the network disappears.
3. **Quiet intelligence:** Auto Mode detects drives and drops ones too short to matter; manual
   Start/Stop is always there.
4. **Private by default:** location history belongs to the user; shared routes hide start and end.
5. **Premium but useful:** visual polish never hides essential driving information.
6. **Content first:** no large titles and no chrome that does not carry information; screens give
   their height to the drive ([screen layout](../../carodo-ios/docs/design-system.md#screen-layout)).

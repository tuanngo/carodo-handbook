Place card v2: one surface, nothing boxed inside

Polish of the card chosen 2026-11-19 (C), after the owner's first look on the iPhone: the card opened as short as its content, read hard, said too little, and boxed every part (figures, visits, address) in a card of its own. v2 opens at a medium height and can be dragged to the top; the figures, the visits and the details sit on the sheet's own ground, apart only by space and hairlines, as Apple Maps' place sheets do. On the Map the card has no map of its own (the real map is right above it); the visits page reached from Journeys, with no map behind it, gets a map header instead. Synthetic places and drives.

The card on the Map

  • One rule for the whole sheet: what is read sits flat on the sheet; nothing is boxed. Sections are apart by space, rows by a hairline that starts at the text. The visit rows stay tappable without a box: each has its route's glyph on the left and a chevron on the right, as Apple's Recents.
  • Height: the card opens at medium (about half the screen) and drags to the top; the group list opens at medium too, however few its rows (a short list read hard on the iPhone; owner, 2026-11-19), and grows to the top when its rows need it. Content past the fold scrolls. Only the Map's two sheets change; forms keep fitting their content (FittedSheetContent).
  • Header: a larger title; the line under it says what the place is in Copper, then the area and province. An unnamed stop: the area is the title, "A place you stopped" the line.
  • Figures in the order the app has today (Latest · Visits · First): a small label above, the value under it, hairlines above and below.
  • Latest visits: date and time on one line ("Tue 11 Nov · 07:42"), "From … · km" on the next, cut to one line (the drive shows it whole). "All 4" moves beside the section's title; the row of its own goes. The route glyph is the Journeys glyph (RouteGlyph) of that drive; it needs the drive's simplified route, read with the visits.
  • Details in place of the last card: Address, Coordinates, Radius as label and value rows.
  • Dragged to the top the sheet stops under the status bar's height plus a strip of the map, so the selected marker stays in view; the card lists every visit there.

The group list, Dark, Vietnamese, the visits page

  • The group list has the card's header: a large title on the left, a round close button on the right (not a "Close" text button in a bar). Each row says what the place is, its area, and its visits ("4 visits · last 11 Nov"); a place no drive ended at says so in its line.
  • The visits page from Journeys (PlaceVisitsView) has no map behind it, so it opens with a map header: a still image of the place (a map snapshot through Core/MapLookup, cached), its name and kind over the image's foot. The rest is the card's content, so the place reads the same wherever it is opened. A live map is not used there: a map nobody pans is a still image.
  • Not in this pass: Save place and Edit place (decided after the polish), the drive's duration in a visit row (needs the drive's moving time read with the visits; add if cheap), duplicates of one detected place (detection).

2 · Making, done, not finished, Dark and Vietnamese, large text

  • The preview is the video: the stage of W4a with the overlay of W4b, as large as the screen allows. Play runs the clip as it will be written (same rate, same camera); the bar's playhead follows, and dragging it scrubs.
  • Header: what will be exported, in the person's own figures: "Whole drive" or "Part of the drive", then distance and time.
  • Tools, left to right. Look: Light or Dark map, Copper or Speed line, Standard or Satellite (opens as Replay had it). Aa: what the frame shows. Privacy zone: on (Copper) or off for this export (no hatched ends then; Settings is not changed).
  • Two steps (owner, 2026-11-13): the editor decides what the video shows; Make Video opens a sheet that decides how long it is (15 s · 30 s · 60 s, each with its rate; 30 s chosen) and starts it. Play in the editor runs at the length last chosen.
  • Aa menu: Date, Vehicle, Distance, Time, Speed, each on or off; the block closes up around what is left. Not offered, by rule: a clock (time is always from 0), the journey's title (titles name places). The CARODO lockup and carodo.app at the end always stay (owner, 2026-11-13).
  • The range bar is Replay's speed profile in its speed colours. Two handles choose the part exported (W4e, drawn here so the screen is designed once). The privacy zone is the hatched ends: the handles stop at them while the zone is on.
  • Make Video: the preview shows each frame as it is written; bar, share done, time left, Cancel; the screen stays awake. Leaving Carodo stops it with no half file ("not finished", Try Again).
  • Done: the finished clip plays in the preview and the share sheet opens once (Save Video, Messages, AirDrop, …); Share opens it again; changing any tool returns to Make Video.
  • A drive shorter than its zone: the preview says so and Make Video is off until the zone is turned off; never the whole route instead.

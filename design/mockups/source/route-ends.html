Route ends — one mark everywhere

Today a route's ends are flags on discs (an Ivory disc with a flag for the start, a Charcoal disc with a chequered flag for the end), plain discs under 18 pt. Proposal (owner, 2026-11-14): the mockup's marks on every surface, maps included: the start is a ring, the end a dot, a loop the two in one. They tell start from end by shape, not colour or a symbol, so they stay readable at 6 pt in a list and at 16 pt on a map, and they read as part of the line rather than pins on top of it. A saved place at an end keeps its place marker on maps, as today.

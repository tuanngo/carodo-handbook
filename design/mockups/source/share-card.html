Share card — the image and its editor

A drive as one image for social apps and messages (carodo-ios docs/specs/share.md). The whole route on a muted map, with the Replay video's block of figures: one look for the image and the video. Cards are 360 points wide, written at 3x (1080 × 1920 and 1080 × 1080). The map here is drawn for the mock-up (no real place); the app uses a map snapshot.

4 · Direction D — the blend (owner, 2026-11-13): the map fills the card, the day is a stamp; Stamp or Simple is a choice

The map is never boxed in. The block stays at the top on its soft ground, which is also the paper the stamp is pressed on. One brand element a card: in Stamp the wheel is inside the stamp, so the lockup is left out and the stamp is the logo; in Simple (direction A) the lockup and a line of text. carodo.app names Carodo in both.

  • D · The stamp is the Passport's round stamp (A · Round stamp, with lettering), unchanged in shape: double ring, the wheel in the centre. Its band carries what the lockup's line carried: the day above, the part of day below. Copper ink, pressed a little off straight, never a clock.
  • D · The line is always Copper (owner, 2026-11-13): never coloured by speed, the colours mean nothing to someone who sees the image. C3 and the strip in section 3 are kept only as the record of what was tried.
  • D · Style is a tool in the editor: Stamp (default) or Simple. Everything else is the same card: the same fit, the same figures, the same privacy zone.

3 · Direction C — a page of the drive journal (after the owner's reference, 2026-11-13)

The reference's strength kept: the map above, the figures on a calm ground under it. Made Carodo's own: the ground is the journal's paper, the map is a window in the page (Apple's logo stays whole inside it), the day is a postmark of the Driving Passport instead of a line of text, and the drive's speed profile is its signature at the foot.

  • C · What is Carodo's and nobody else's: the postmark (the Passport's stamp family: day and part of day, never a clock), Copper ink on ivory paper, the speed profile strip of Replay, and later the stamp of a badge earned on this drive.
  • C · Left out on purpose: start and end clock times (P9), a top speed (only if the person adds it, later), accent colour swatches (one brand ink), names of places written by Carodo.
  • C · Tools: size, paper (Ivory or Charcoal), line (Copper or Speed), privacy zone. Share is the Copper button; Save beside it writes straight to Photos (needs the add-only Photos permission: an Info.plist key, the owner's approval).
  • The block is at the top (A, recommended): the same block as a frame of the Replay video (lockup and carodo.app, day and part of day, vehicle, distance, duration); Apple's logo and Legal link stay clear at the foot; apps that show a 9:16 image full screen cover its foot more than its head. B is drawn only to compare: the block covers Apple's logo there.
  • The map is fitted to the route, not the route to the map: the route (after the privacy zone is cut) is fitted into the area the block leaves free (the dashed box in 2), north up, with a floor on the zoom so a very short drive never shows at house level. Nothing of the route is ever under the block.
  • The line: the real route on its roads, drawn with the Feed shape's soft curve (Catmull-Rom through the points, round ends), Copper on a casing of the ground colour. Not the Feed's shape itself: that one is turned and mirrored on purpose and matches no road.
  • Privacy zone on (the person's setting, 300 m by default): the first and last metres are cut and the line simply ends, with no marker. Off for this card: the whole route with the start and end markers of the design system.
  • No map (offline, nothing cached): the same card on the plain ground, so a card can always be made without a network.
  • Never on a card: a clock time, the journey's title, a place's name or symbol written by Carodo (the map's own labels stay), a stop, a speed.
  • The editor: the preview is the image. Three tools: size (9:16, 1:1), look (Light or Dark map), privacy zone (shown only when the setting is not Off). The image is ready at once, so the one button is Share: the system sheet with Save Image, Messages, AirDrop.

Place card: a place on the Map, its visits, its journeys

Chosen 2026-11-19: C (names under the markers, a tap opens the place card); figures Visits · Last · First; three latest visits; a detected place can be renamed, its name the person's own; Open in Maps later (nice to have). Tapping a place on the Map today opens MapKit's callout with hosted SwiftUI lines. It opens oversized on the first tap (blank space above the title; reproduced on the simulator 2026-11-19), names a place only through Apple's point of interest or the cached area, and reaches the drives only through a button to the visits page. Places will be used to find a stop again, so the place deserves a surface of its own. Three directions, then the states of the recommended one. Synthetic places and drives.

Directions

  • A keeps MapKit's callout, made compact (name, what it is · area, visits) and sized right; the whole callout opens the visits page. Cheapest, but the callout stays MapKit's: it covers the map around the place, sizes hosted views late, and holds no list.
  • B replaces the callout with a place card (recommended): a sheet over the map at a medium height, the marker enlarged and the map slid so it stays above the card. The card is the place: name, what it is, address, three figures, its latest visits (each opens that journey's Journey Detail), all visits, and Save as a place for a detected one. Dragged up, it is the visits page itself: one surface, not a callout then a page. Our own SwiftUI: no hosted-view sizing, Dynamic Type and VoiceOver as every sheet.
  • C is B plus names on the map: past street level (about 3 km across) each detected place carries its name under the marker, and a group says "+2 more", as the reference shows. Finding a stop again is reading the map; a tap is still the card. Labels cost MapKit collisions: they follow the same displayPriority as the markers (saved, visited often, once).

States of the place card

  • What it is comes from Apple's point-of-interest category (MKPointOfInterestCategory) when Apple matched the place to a point of interest; Carodo maps 13 of Apple's categories to a symbol and a name today, the others draw a pin with no kind. Apple's answer is never stored, only cached (a week), so offline with nothing cached the card says what Carodo knows itself.
  • Offline the card is the same card, never MapKit's default: the name falls back to the cached area, else "A place you stopped"; the figures and the visits come from the device and are always there. Nothing waits on the network.
  • A saved place shows its role's colour and symbol, Private area when it is private, and Edit instead of Save. Its visits list its journeys the same way.
  • Rename (owner, 2026-11-19, a): a detected place stays detected with the person's own name (places.name), which wins over Apple's and shows offline. The field starts empty, Apple's name only its placeholder; saving Apple's name unchanged stores nothing (Apple Map Data is cached 7 days, never stored). Clearing it returns to Apple's name, the area, or "A place you stopped".

Each frame below is a real frame of the renderer built in W4a (sample drive, 1080 × 1920), with the proposed overlay drawn on it. Shown at 360 × 640 points. The overlay follows the map: ivory on a Light map, charcoal on a Dark one; Copper only in the speed arc and the mark. Apple's logo and Legal link stay clear in every layout. No clock, no place name, no start or end marker of a saved place.

3 · The opening and the arrival (privacy zone on)

The video opens on the whole trimmed route and ends a second after the last point kept: the first and last 300 m are never drawn, the distance and the time count from 0 at the first point kept, and the flag is where the trimmed route ends, not where the car parked.

  • Figures: distance so far (counts up), time so far ("14 min", "1 h 12 min"; from 0, never a clock), the speed dial (Replay's own, at the drive's recorded speed).
  • The drive's line: the local day and part of day (as the share card), then the vehicle's name. No journey title: titles often carry place names.
  • The mark: the CARODO lockup, small, never over the wheel.
  • Not shown: the clock, the scrubber, the rate, speed-band legend, place names of the app (the map's own street labels remain), saved-place markers (Home, Work).

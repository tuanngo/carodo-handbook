Driving Passport button: four sketches

The button sits where the avatar was (top of Journeys and Feed). The avatar stays the public identity; the stamp is the private journal. Each card: large (the Passport header), the 44 pt nav button, and 20 px.

A · Round stamp

Classic entry stamp: double ring, lettering in the band. The most "passport" at large size; at nav size it is a ringed mark.

B · Visa stamp

Tilted rectangle with a dashed rule, the month inked inside. Playful, but the tilt fights a round bar button.

C · Booklet

The passport itself: charcoal cover, copper mark. Reads as "my book" and is the clearest at 20 px. Ivory cover in Dark.

D · Month postmark

A postmark for the current month. Inked once the month has a drive, faded before: the button tells you the month is "stamped".
